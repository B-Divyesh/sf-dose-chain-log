A timing log, not clinical guidance.

Important: Dose Chain Log records what you tell it. It does not recommend medicines, verify doses, detect interactions, or decide whether or when a medicine is safe to take.

Use of the app

Use the app only for medicines already prescribed or chosen with a qualified professional, and follow their instructions. You are responsible for checking the event you record and keeping your own backup if needed.

Emergencies

Do not rely on this app for urgent care. For urgent symptoms, suspected overdose, or an emergency, contact local emergency services or poison control immediately.

Reminder limitations

Notifications can be delayed or suppressed by the browser, Android battery settings, device shutdown, permission changes, or other system conditions. Always use an appropriate backup plan when missing a dose reminder could cause harm.
